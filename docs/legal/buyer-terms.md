# Buyer Terms — draft

Effective date: `[LEGAL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED]`

## 1. Marketplace role

Resaller helps buyers discover products and communicate with independent sellers. In the approved MVP, Resaller does not collect payment, hold escrow or settle seller funds. Unless the final legal review determines otherwise, the product sale is between buyer and the seller identified on the order.

## 2. Account

Buyers must provide accurate information, keep credentials confidential and immediately report unauthorized use. Automated access, account sale, identity misuse, abuse, fraud and attempts to bypass authorization are prohibited.

## 3. Orders

A multi-seller checkout creates a separate order for each store. Product, variant, quantity, price, seller, policies, delivery facts and legal versions are snapshotted at checkout. A seller has 60 minutes to accept. Rejection or timeout releases reserved stock. Repeated submission does not create duplicate orders when the same idempotency key is used.

## 4. Payment and delivery

The MVP does not process payment. Buyer and seller use only lawful payment/delivery arrangements clearly shown or agreed in the authorized order channel. Users must never send card PINs, passwords, one-time codes or unrelated identity documents through chat.

## 5. Cancellation, return and dispute

Before seller acceptance, an eligible buyer may cancel directly. Afterwards, cancellation is a request governed by order status and applicable law. Returns and disputes follow the published Returns and Disputes Policy and any mandatory consumer rights. A platform workflow does not reduce a statutory right.

## 6. Content and reviews

Questions and reviews must be truthful, relevant and non-abusive. A verified review requires a completed order. Resaller may label, restrict or remove unlawful/manipulative content and records the moderation reason. Imported testimonials are labelled and excluded from verified ratings.

## 7. Availability and responsibility

Listings are seller-provided. Resaller applies verification, moderation and risk controls but does not guarantee uninterrupted availability or that every seller statement is correct. Nothing in these terms excludes liability or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 8. Enforcement and changes

Resaller may restrict an account to protect users, comply with law or investigate abuse, with reasons and audit records where appropriate. Material term changes are versioned; old order snapshots remain unchanged.
