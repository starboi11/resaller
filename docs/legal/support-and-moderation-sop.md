# Support and Moderation SOP — operational draft

Owner: `[OPERATIONS_OWNER]`

Emergency contact: `[SECURITY_CONTACT]`
Status: owner approval and live rehearsal required

## 1. Intake and identity

Support accepts in-app tickets and, at launch, approved email/Telegram channels. Every external message becomes a ticket. Operators verify the requester before exposing order, seller, security or personal information. Never request passwords, OTP codes, card PINs or unnecessary identity documents.

## 2. Priority

- P0: active safety threat, confirmed credential leak, major breach or illegal-content emergency — acknowledge immediately, page security/owner and preserve evidence.
- P1: account takeover, high-risk fraud, widespread outage, malware upload or serious prohibited listing — initial response target 1 hour.
- P2: order/dispute, seller verification, return or moderation appeal — initial response target 1 business day.
- P3: general question, UX issue or suggestion — initial response target 2 business days.

Targets are operational goals, not legal deadlines; mandatory deadlines override them.

## 3. Least privilege

Use the minimum role required. Verification admins view verification documents; dispute admins view dispute evidence; security admins investigate security events. Exceptional access needs a legitimate ticket/reason. Never share admin sessions or download sensitive files to unmanaged devices.

## 4. Moderation decision

1. Confirm scope, role and conflict of interest.
2. Review the exact listing/content, relevant history and policy version.
3. Separate automated risk signals from verified facts.
4. Choose the least restrictive effective action.
5. Record policy basis, evidence and a clear reason in the audit trail.
6. Notify the affected user without exposing reporters or security controls.
7. Provide the approved appeal route.

## 5. Disputes

Operators do not alter evidence or promise a payment reversal the platform cannot perform. Check immutable order snapshot, timeline, chat, policy versions and both parties' evidence. Escalate conflicts, threats, suspected crime and legally complex outcomes to the owner/counsel.

## 6. Security and privacy incidents

Stop further exposure, preserve logs, rotate affected credentials, revoke sessions, assess impacted data/users, notify the owner and follow the counsel-approved incident notification process. Do not speculate publicly. Keep a timestamped incident record and post-incident corrective plan.

## 7. Quality control

High-risk actions use confirmation dialogs, mandatory reasons and audit logs. The operations owner reviews a sample weekly, role assignments monthly and access inventory at least annually or sooner after staff changes. Metrics include first response, resolution time, reopen rate, appeal overturn rate, backlog age and security-event review age.

## 8. Launch rehearsal

Before production, rehearse one seller verification, prohibited listing, account recovery, order dispute, malware upload, privacy request, security incident and appeal. Record who acted, response time, decision, evidence and improvement item.
