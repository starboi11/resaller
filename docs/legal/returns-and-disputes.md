# Returns and Disputes Policy — draft

Effective date: `[LEGAL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED]`

## 1. Mandatory rights first

This policy is a workflow, not a waiver. Mandatory rights under applicable consumer, civil and retail law prevail. The final periods, exceptions and refund mechanics require counsel approval. Relevant official references include the Civil Code and Cabinet Resolution No. 75 retail rules.

## 2. Before acceptance

An eligible buyer may cancel while the order is waiting for seller acceptance. Stock is released. The seller may reject, or the system may time out after 60 minutes.

## 3. After acceptance

A buyer submits a cancellation/return request with an accurate reason and evidence. The seller receives a deadline shown in the order workspace. Direct cancellation is unavailable where fulfilment has progressed; this does not remove a mandatory legal remedy.

## 4. Return conditions

The seller evaluates whether the product is defective, misdescribed, incomplete, damaged, wrongly delivered or otherwise subject to a legal remedy. For a quality product, condition, use, seals, labels, proof of purchase and any statutory non-returnable category are considered only as allowed by law. A seller must not invent a restriction that conflicts with mandatory law.

## 5. Evidence

Acceptable evidence may include order records, photos/video, serial/variant information, delivery records and authorized chat. Never upload passwords, payment PINs, excessive identity data or unrelated private material. Files are private, malware-scanned and available only to authorized participants/operators.

## 6. Dispute stages

1. Buyer and seller try to resolve the issue in order chat.
2. A party opens a dispute and states the requested outcome.
3. Both sides receive a reasonable evidence deadline.
4. A dispute operator reviews the order snapshot, policies, timeline, chat and evidence.
5. The operator records a reasoned outcome and audit entry.

Possible platform outcomes are guidance, return accepted/rejected, replacement/repair recommendation, cancellation status correction, seller/listing moderation or no platform action. Since the MVP does not hold funds, Resaller cannot technically reverse or release payment; the liable seller/payment party must execute any legally required refund.

## 7. Appeals and authorities

The final appeal window and escalation contact are `[LEGAL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED]`. Users retain any non-waivable right to contact competent consumer-protection bodies or courts. Resaller preserves relevant records subject to the retention schedule and lawful requests.
