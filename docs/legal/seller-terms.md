# Seller Terms — draft

Effective date: `[LEGAL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED]`

## 1. Eligibility and verification

Sellers must be legally permitted to trade, provide accurate identity/business documents, maintain current contacts and complete required tax, licence, certification and consumer-information duties. Resaller may request reverification and restrict a store while evidence is reviewed.

## 2. Marketplace scope and fees

The MVP sales commission is 0%. Payment collection, escrow, settlement and payouts are not provided. Premium/subscription payments are deferred. Any future fee requires separate disclosure, acceptance and legal/tax implementation; it cannot retroactively change existing orders.

## 3. Listings

The seller is responsible for lawful ownership/source, accurate title and description, current price and stock, genuine media, condition, variants, safety warnings, warranty, delivery promise and category-specific documents. Hidden fees, counterfeit goods, manipulated reviews, misleading discounts and prohibited listings are forbidden.

## 4. Orders

Stock must be available when listed. A seller must accept or reject an incoming order within 60 minutes. Acceptance creates a fulfilment commitment. The seller must use valid status transitions, communicate delays and preserve all order communication in the authorized workspace. Seller edits never rewrite an existing order snapshot.

## 5. Payment, receipts, tax and delivery

Because Resaller does not collect payment in the MVP, the seller remains responsible for lawful collection arrangements, fiscal receipts/invoices, tax reporting, delivery evidence, warranties, refunds and other mandatory seller duties. Counsel must confirm the exact marketplace/operator fiscal model before launch.

## 6. Buyer data

Seller and team members may use buyer data only to fulfil, support or legally document that order. Marketing reuse, sale, scraping, unrelated contact and credential sharing are prohibited. Access must be removed promptly when a team member leaves.

## 7. Returns, disputes and moderation

Sellers must follow mandatory consumer law and the published Returns and Disputes Policy. They must answer evidence requests honestly and may not retaliate against a buyer. Resaller may pause listings, restrict stores, preserve evidence and refer suspected illegality to authorities.

## 8. Enforcement

Risk signals trigger human review and are not automatic fraud verdicts. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to listing removal, store restriction, suspension or closure. High-risk actions require an authorized operator, recorded reason and audit trail. Appeal/contact route: `[SELLER_SUPPORT_EMAIL]`.
