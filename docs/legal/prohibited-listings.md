# Prohibited Listings Policy — draft

Effective date: `[LEGAL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED]`

## 1. Always prohibited

- Illegal, stolen, counterfeit or fraudulently obtained goods.
- Weapons, explosives, ammunition or components where listing/sale is unlawful or unsafe.
- Narcotics, controlled substances, toxic materials and instructions primarily enabling illegal harm.
- Human organs, human trafficking, sexual exploitation and content involving minors.
- Malware, stolen credentials, unauthorized access services, financial-account access or identity documents.
- Personal data sold or disclosed without lawful authority.
- Hate, extremist/terrorist material or goods promoting unlawful violence.
- Products that infringe intellectual-property rights.
- Fake reviews, deceptive “investment” offers, pyramid schemes or guaranteed-profit claims.
- Any product recalled, prohibited from circulation or lacking a legally mandatory licence/certificate/marking.

## 2. Restricted or approval-required

Medicines/medical devices, alcohol/tobacco/nicotine products, food, cosmetics, chemicals, animals/plants, precious metals/stones, age-restricted goods, telecom/radio equipment, financial services, digital goods and other regulated categories may be blocked entirely in MVP or enabled only after counsel confirms licences, age gates, delivery, advertising, receipt and recordkeeping controls.

## 3. Seller duties

The seller must verify legality before listing and provide requested evidence. “Other sellers offer it” is not evidence of legality. Misclassification, coded language, external links or image-only descriptions may not be used to bypass controls.

## 4. Enforcement

Resaller may reject, restrict or remove a listing; preserve evidence; limit the store/account; and report suspected illegality where required. Automated signals only prioritize review. Final high-risk moderation requires an authorized human operator and recorded reason. Appeals go to `[MODERATION_APPEAL_EMAIL]` within the final counsel-approved period.
