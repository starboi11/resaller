## 1. Scope and roles

This policy covers Resaller accounts, marketplace browsing, seller verification, stores, listings, carts, orders, messages, reviews, support, moderation and security records. Resaller acts as the platform operator. Sellers may independently determine how they use buyer information needed to fulfil an order and must provide their own notices where required.

## 2. Data we process

- Account and authentication: username, password hash, sessions, security events and optional two-factor records.
- Buyer operations: cart, saved address, order, delivery, cancellation, return, dispute and review records.
- Seller operations: identity/business verification data, store profile, team permissions, catalog, stock and fulfilment history.
- Communications: order chat, attachments, Q&A, support tickets and notification endpoints.
- Technical and safety data: request IDs, timestamps, hashed IP/security signals, device metadata, audit logs and rate-limit events.
- AI assistant: selected mode, authorized source identifiers, outcome and a one-way prompt hash. The MVP does not store the raw assistant prompt in the audit log and does not send it to an external model provider.

We do not intentionally collect payment card data in the approved MVP because payment collection and settlement are deferred.

## 3. Why we process data

We process data to create and secure accounts; provide marketplace, order and communication functions; verify sellers; prevent abuse; moderate content; answer support requests; comply with legal duties; establish or defend claims; and improve reliability using aggregated or de-identified information.

## 4. Sharing

Data is shared only as necessary with the relevant buyer or seller, authorized store team members, vetted hosting/storage/notification/security providers, and public authorities where lawfully required. Public listings, store profiles, Q&A and verified reviews are public by design. Verification documents, private chat and dispute evidence are not public.

## 5. Storage and international processing

Public and private media are separated. Access is least-privilege, logged and protected in transit. The final hosting location, cross-border safeguards, database registration/localization requirements and each processor must be approved by counsel before launch. No provider may use Resaller data for model training unless the owner and users are lawfully informed and the required consent/legal basis exists.

## 6. Retention

We retain data only for operational, security, accounting, evidence and legal periods approved in the final retention schedule. Account deletion does not erase records that must remain for completed orders, disputes, fraud prevention, audit integrity or mandatory retention; those records are restricted and deleted or anonymized when the applicable period ends.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, a user may request access, correction, restriction, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, account closure and deletion of eligible data. Requests require identity verification and are sent to `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`. Users may manage notification preferences; essential order/security notices cannot be disabled while the service is active.

## 8. Security and incidents

Resaller uses role-based authorization, session controls, admin 2FA, encryption for sensitive endpoints, malware scanning, private object storage, immutable audit records, monitoring and backups. No system is risk-free. Material incidents are investigated, contained, documented and notified as required by law.

## 9. Children

The minimum permitted age, parental-consent rule and seller age requirement must be set by counsel before publication. Until then, the service must not knowingly onboard a child as a seller.

## 10. Changes

Material changes receive a new version and effective date. Where required, users must accept the new version before continuing affected actions. Historical orders retain the legal version accepted at checkout.
