## Severity-ordered gaps

### P0 — required before closed beta

- Seller product creation through admin approval must work as one tested journey.
- Buyer product selection, multi-seller checkout and order creation must work as one tested journey.
- Seller acceptance, rejection, timeout and fulfillment transitions must be clear and browser-tested.
- Buyer completion, cancellation/change, return, dispute and verified review must be browser-tested.
- Private chat authorization, unread/reconnect and attachment failures must be tested.
- Admin verification, listing moderation and dispute decisions need complete operator screens and tests.

### P1 — required before production

- Approved visual screenshot comparison for desktop/mobile home, search, filter, product, order and chat.
- Seller wizard autosave, real preview, media reorder and variant media.
- Search language normalization, typo/synonym behavior and pagination.
- Firefox/WebKit, representative phone sizes, accessibility and visual regression evidence.
- Live object storage, notifications, malware scanning, monitoring, backup/restore and rollback.
- Staging load and penetration tests.

### P2 — launch inputs

- Owner/legal-approved policies.
- Support and moderation procedures.
- Closed beta with 20–50 sellers and fixes from observed behavior.
- Production deployment, DNS and HTTPS after the launch gates pass.

## AI assistant boundary

The assistant can help search and compare public products, explain order status, and guide sellers through forms. It must use server-authorized tools, minimize personal data, treat catalog/user content as untrusted, rate-limit usage and audit sensitive access. It cannot directly approve listings, change stock, transition orders, resolve disputes or manage roles. A production model/provider remains a cost, retention and privacy decision.
