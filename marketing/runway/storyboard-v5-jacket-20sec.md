# Resaller 20-second ad — jacket concept

Storyboard: `storyboard-v5-jacket-20sec.png`

Product continuity: one charcoal-black cropped autumn jacket with a crimson inner-lining detail. Buyer, seller, courier, box and red tape must remain consistent in every shot. Generated blank device screens are placeholders for real Resaller.uz captures; real brand mark must be composited on the courier's chest patch in post-production, never generated by the video model.

Voice: one warm, confident Uzbek narrator at conversational pace; record cleanly and mix below foreground dialogue/effects. Avoid synthetic-sounding pronunciation, unsupported delivery-time promises, and any payment flow not actually available. Music: understated cinematic pulse, not an oversized trailer; leave room for voice. Final end card: real logo; “Xarid qilish” and “Sotishni boshlash”.

Production gate: the courier uniform and voice must reflect the actual fulfillment arrangement. Do not publish a scene that implies Resaller operates its own courier fleet unless that is true at launch.

## Runway Gen-4 Turbo generation plan

Generate four separate 5-second, 16:9 image-to-video clips (25 credits each; minimum 100 credits before retries). Use full-frame key images matching each shot, not the entire storyboard sheet. Review each result for face, jacket and parcel continuity before moving to the next. The current Runway account showed 0 credits on 2026-09-26, so these clips have not been generated.

1. Buyer/product: “The same Uzbek woman in a cream sweater naturally scrolls a smartphone while seated by a Tashkent apartment window. Slow dolly from her face to the phone, realistic hand movement, restrained cinematic morning light. Keep the phone display blank and stable for a real Resaller.uz screen composite; no generated text, UI or logo.”
2. Seller/packing: “The same seller receives the order, checks the same charcoal cropped jacket with crimson inner lining, folds it carefully and places it into a kraft box with one thin red stripe. Continuous natural motion, tactile fabric, no camera shake, no extra products appearing, laptop display blank for real-site composite.”
3. Courier/handoff: “The same courier in matte-black uniform takes the same red-striped box from the seller and delivers it to the same buyer at her apartment doorway. Realistic box size and hand movements; hold the courier chest patch stable and blank for the actual emblem in post. Do not add any brand marks or words.”
4. Final/payoff: “The buyer wears the same charcoal cropped jacket over her cream sweater and adjusts the lapel in a mirror, relaxed confident expression. Camera eases back into elegant dark negative space on the right for the actual Resaller logo and two CTA labels in post. Preserve black fabric, zipper and crimson lining detail; no generated text.”

Post-production: record the actual checkout and seller-order screens; track them onto blank device displays. Composite the real vector Resaller mark onto the courier uniform, then add final title, professionally recorded Uzbek voiceover, music and foley.
